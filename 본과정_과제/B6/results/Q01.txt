==============================================================================
Q1. [기본 조회] 정가 20,000원 이상인 도서를 비싼 순서로 5권만 보기  (WHERE + ORDER BY + LIMIT)
==============================================================================

[SQL]
SELECT book_id, title, author, price
FROM book
WHERE price >= 20000
ORDER BY price DESC, title
LIMIT 5;

+---------+----------------------------------+-------------------+-------+
